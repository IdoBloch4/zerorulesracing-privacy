Zero Rules Racing: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Zero Rules Racing ("the game") is made by Ido Bloch ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and how you can ask us to delete it. The short version: we collect only what the game needs to work and to get better. There are no ads, no advertising ID, no location, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What we collect

Game account and progress. When you first open the game, it creates an anonymous player account for you (no email or sign-in needed). Linked to that account we keep your nickname, level, XP, coins, unlocked looks, streaks and daily rewards, so your progress is saved.

Your nickname. You choose it, and other players see it in races and on results screens. Please don't use your real name.

Game activity. Our servers record what happens in the game: when you play, races you start and finish, your places, coins earned and in-game items bought with coins, your level and the app version. We use this to run the game, keep races fair, and understand what players enjoy.

Diagnostics. If the game crashes or a race fails, or if you tap "Send logs", the game sends a report with recent game log lines, a screenshot of the game, your device model, operating system version and memory size, and a short session code. We use these only to fix bugs.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the game receives a push token from Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging so it can send reminders (such as your daily reward) and game news. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising ID and no ads of any kind.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone for advertising or marketing.

Who processes the data

The game runs on these services, which process data only to provide them to us:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies): player accounts, saved progress and the game's server logic.
  • Edgegap: online race servers. Your device connects to a race server over the internet for the length of a race.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering notifications.
  • GitHub (private repository): storing bug reports.

All data is sent encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

Bots

Some racers in a race are computer-controlled bots. Bots have no personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep game data while your account is active. Bug reports are kept for up to 12 months. Analytics are kept in summarized form for as long as they are useful.

Deleting your data

To delete your data, email idobloch@guestim.com with your nickname (shown in the game's settings). We will delete your account, saved progress, game activity and reports within 30 days and reply when it's done. Uninstalling the game removes the data on your phone.

Children

The game is made for everyone aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will delete their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
